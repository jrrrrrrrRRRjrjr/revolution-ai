1. Introduction
(R)Evolution is an AI-based relationship analysis app that analyzes users' actual 'conversation data' to resolve anxiety during relationships and objectively review emotions after breakups to facilitate personal growth.

2. The Problem
Existing relationship counseling tends to be stereotypical or stays at a general level. This app solves two core problems:

"During a relationship": "When feeling anxious not knowing what the other person is thinking" (e.g., feeling like only I'm interested, feeling like their feelings have cooled), users rely on emotional speculation without objective evidence (MBTI, internet searches).

"After a breakup": Users fall into excessive 'self-blame' by unilaterally attributing the cause of the breakup to themselves. This is because they are "emotionally too distressed" to make objective situational judgments.

3. Core Principles
This app absolutely adheres to the following principles based on Jo's feedback:

Logic First (No-Fluff): Eliminates "trying to look impressive" jargon or "insincere AI" stereotypical comfort.

Evidence-Based (Data-Driven): All analyses present 'actual conversation logs' uploaded by users as 'evidence'.

Suggestive Tone (Non-Directive): Uses a 'friendly suggestion' tone that allows users to realize things themselves, rather than "forcing words".

User-Specific (Personalized): All responses and suggestions are based on the user's MBTI, age gap, gender, and 'hobbies' (e.g., screen baseball, gaming) extracted from conversation logs.

4. MVP Feature Requirements
[Common Feature: Onboarding]
Mode Selection: Choose between "In a relationship" or "Breakup" mode.

Information Input:

Self: Age, gender, MBTI

Partner: Age, gender, MBTI

Relationship: Relationship duration, long-distance (LDR) status

Data Upload: Upload KakaoTalk/text message conversation logs (.txt).

[Mode 1: 'In a Relationship' Features]
Goal: Diagnose the user's anxiety with 'data' and suggest 'personalized actions'.

1.1. Relationship Dashboard (Data Diagnosis)

Requirements: Analyze conversation logs to display relationship health status in objective metrics.

Essential Items:

Meeting proposal ratio (self/partner)

Average response speed (self/partner)

UX: All data must be presented together with actual conversation logs as 'evidence'.

1.2. Attachment Style Diagnosis (Combination Analysis)

Requirements: Diagnose both people's tendencies (e.g., anxious type, avoidant type) based on conversation data.

Essential Items: Along with diagnosis results, briefly explain the risks of that 'combination' (e.g., 'chase-escape' vicious cycle).

1.3. 24/7 AI Relationship Coach

Requirements: When users request help with anxiety like "It feels like only I like them", AI 'boldly' diagnoses based on conversation logs.

Positive Diagnosis: "According to data, your partner is expressing high interest in Jo. (Evidence: Used 'I love you' 8 times)"

Negative Diagnosis: "According to data, Jo's feeling that 'we don't seem like lovers' is likely true. (Evidence: Meeting avoidance with 'why bother' remarks, suspicious 'lying' behavior detected)"

Action Suggestion: For 'negative' diagnoses, 'suggest' approaching like a 'casual friend' based on partner's 'interests' (e.g., baseball, malatang).

[Mode 2: 'Breakup' Features]
Goal: Reduce "self-blame" and help users realize on their own "Oh, it wasn't just my fault" through objective 'evidence'.

2.1. Interactive Emotional Cause Analysis

Requirements: AI asks questions and users input emotions in 'descriptive' format. (No multiple choice)

Exception Handling: Only when users hesitate to input, provide 'recommended keywords' (e.g., resentment, betrayal).

Analysis: AI analyzes the cause of inputted emotions by connecting them with data (e.g., effort imbalance, unilateral notification).

2.2. Objective Relationship Review (Mutual Fault)

Requirements: Present both sides' behavioral patterns under the title "Breakup Cause Analysis: Mutual Fault".

UX: List 'partner's behavioral patterns' and 'Jo's behavioral patterns'.

Evidence Presentation (Required): Evidence for each pattern must be shown as 'actual conversation log' snippets uploaded by users in a 'dropdown' format.

2.3. Preparation for Next Relationship (Future Suggestion)

Requirements: 'Suggest' future actions based on past data.

Example: "Since Jo desires a 'meaningful' relationship, it would be good to consider a 'secure type' partner with smooth communication in your next relationship."

5. Out of Scope (MVP v1.0 Out of Scope)
According to Jo's feedback, the following features are excluded from MVP for clarity:

Ambiguous "warning signal alerts"

"Emotional state sharing" that is difficult to objectify

"Communication translator" that seems too AI-like and lacks persuasiveness
